Changed:

- templates/base.html
- templates/batch_management.html (new)

ZI and Administrator can archive/restore batches.
Archiving sets member status to Archived but does not delete member records or linked history.
Each member's previous status is saved and restored.
Actions are recorded with actor, role, batch, count and timestamp.
Schema tables are created automatically at startup by init_db().

Before production use:
1. Back up the PostgreSQL database.
2. Deploy and confirm Batch Management appears for ZI/Admin only.
3. Check the stored batch value for 2025 Batch C in the batch register before archiving.
4. Test with a non-production batch/database copy first.
